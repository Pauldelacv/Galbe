# Code of Conduct

Galbe is made by and for designers, artists and developers from many backgrounds. We want
every space of the project — issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events — to be
welcoming, constructive and free of harassment.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people working in a second language.
- Giving feedback on work, not on people; being specific and actionable.
- Crediting others' work, and respecting licences and creators' rights.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about identity, background or ability.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sharing pirated assets, or content that infringes someone else's rights.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/Pauldelacv/Galbe/security/advisories/new)
marked "Code of Conduct" (it is only visible to maintainers). Reports are handled
confidentially and promptly. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour breaks these rules, in proportion to the harm caused.
